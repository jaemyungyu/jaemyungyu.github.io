I am a Research Scientist at NAVER AI Lab. My research focuses on efficient training and reasoning in large language models, with an emphasis on optimization methods. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from KAIST in 2025, advised by Prof. Junmo Kim. I welcome research collaborations in these areas.
